## Pillar + Cluster architecture

One clear pillar emerged from this session's data: **e-bike law/legality in Australia**. Three
closely related keywords cluster on this single topic (same search intent — "is my e-bike
legal/what are the rules") and should NOT be split into three posts — that would be the exact
cannibalization pattern the skill prohibits.

**Pillar post: "Electric Bike Laws in Australia: The Complete State-by-State Guide"**
- Target keywords: electric bike laws qld (1,000/26), electric bike legislation (1,000/52),
  electric bikes and the law (1,000/47) — all merged into one 3,000w+ pillar
- Meta description: "Electric bike laws vary by state in Australia. This guide covers power
  limits, registration, helmet rules and where you can ride — updated for 2026."
- Slug: `/blog/electric-bike-laws-australia/`
- H2/H3 outline:
  - H2: Is my e-bike road-legal in Australia? (power/speed limit basics — 250W continuous, 25km/h assist cutoff)
  - H2: State-by-state rules
    - H3: Queensland
    - H3: New South Wales
    - H3: Victoria
    - H3: Western Australia
    - H3: South Australia / Tasmania / ACT / NT
  - H2: Do I need a licence or registration?
  - H2: Helmet and safety requirements
  - H2: What happens if my e-bike exceeds the legal power limit?
  - H2: FAQ (see faq-bank.md — merge the 3–5 most relevant Qs here)
- Internal links: to Electric Bikes category, to each e-bike subcategory product range, to the
  compliance-focused product specs (continuous wattage figures already on product pages)
- CTA: "Shop road-legal electric bikes" → `/shop/electric-bikes/`
- Schema: Article + FAQPage
- Funnel position: Top (informational, pre-purchase research stage)
